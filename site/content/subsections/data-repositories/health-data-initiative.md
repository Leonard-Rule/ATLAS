## Health Data Initiative (HDI)
id: Health-data-initiative-hdi
type: Reference

The purpose of the HDI is to support data-driven health policy and research for the benefit of Arkansans. It was authorized under the Arkansas General Assembly Act 1035 of 2003. It links Arkansas health related data from state agencies. Only ACHI has access to this data without special requests. The data housed in the HDI includes data from the Arkansas Department of Health (birth and death records, hospital discharge data, emergency department discharge data, Medicare member and claims files from ResDAC, Medicaid member and claims, Delta Dental member names and addresses, and Arkansas Crash data.
To link records within the HDI we use PID.
